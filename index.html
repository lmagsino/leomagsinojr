Open source

A light software factory for coding agents: PRD in, reviewed draft PRs out. You settle the decisions and approve the plan; agents build unattended, a second vendor reviews, and the line stops at a draft PR. Merging stays human — enforced by hooks, not prompts.

Three-Pass Review
Open source

A code review process you install in every repo: Copilot on every push, a light AI review on every PR, and on risky PRs an AI reviewer brief — the change, its risks, where to look first — before two people sign off. AI reads every line; people own the risk.
